# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Settings page (Options → AddOns → Forever Hide Names): choose whether chat bubbles and damage numbers are hidden together with the names. Both are on by default.
- `/names bubbles`, `/names damage` and `/names options`.
- Changing a setting while everything is hidden applies right away.

## 0.1.0

- First version.
- Hiding the interface (Alt+Z) also hides every name, nameplate, chat bubble and damage number over targets; showing it again restores your own settings exactly.
- Nameplates switch right after combat if the key is pressed during a fight.
- Settings survive a crash, logout or /reload while hidden.
- `/names` toggles the names by hand.
- English and German.
